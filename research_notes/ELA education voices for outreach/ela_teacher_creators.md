## Objective: Master list of 15–25 secondary ELA teacher-creators (profile per creator)

### Takeaway
The space splits into two groups. One is a few **mass-reach TikTok comedy/personality accounts**: Claudine James, Mr. Sloma, Molly Dugan and SL Rockfish, each with roughly 1M+ followers but little or no published writing pedagogy or AI stance. The other is a deeper bench of **writing-pedagogy bloggers, TpT sellers and podcasters**: Christina Schneider, Meredith Dobbs, Melissa Kruse, Jenna Copper, Lindsay Ann, Ashley Bible, Amanda Cardenas, Betsy Potash, Jennifer Gonzalez, Dave Stuart Jr., Marcus Luther and Jen Roberts. Their raw audiences are smaller or unverified, but their content closely matches YAWP!'s features. The second group is the better first target for relationship-building.
