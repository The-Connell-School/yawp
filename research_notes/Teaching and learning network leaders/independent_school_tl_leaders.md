# Teaching-and-learning network leaders at US independent schools (beyond Cerenzia/EA CTL and St. Andrew's CTTL)

Method note (read first): research done 27 Nov 2026 by web search only. Page fetching was blocked in this session (toddleapp.com and tanginstitute.andover.edu would not resolve), so every finding below comes from search-result snippets and summaries, not full page reads. Titles marked "per Toddle 100" come from Toddle's "Meet The 100 – Independent School Innovators 2026" list as shown in search results; I could not open the list itself to check spelling or get the full roster. Treat job titles as "to verify on the school site before outreach." I invented no reach figures: where no number was found, reach is marked "unverified."

Connection to the covered models: Toddle's 2026 "The 100" list includes the "Buckley Executive Director, Center for Teaching & Learning, The Episcopal Academy" (Justin Cerenzia's role), so Cerenzia sits in the same 2026 honoree cohort as several people below — [Toddle, Meet The 100 (via search)](https://www.toddleapp.com/the-100/meet-the-100/).

## Q1. Which independent schools have named centers for teaching and learning or research-based institutes, and who leads them?

### Takeaway
Of the schools named in the brief, only a few have verifiable named centers with a public leader: Westminster Schools (Atlanta) Center for Teaching (Danette Morton), Andover's Tang Institute (Eric Roland, interim), Sidwell Friends' Center for Teaching and Learning (a coordinator is public; no director found), Mount Vernon's MVIFI, now Mount Vernon Ventures, and Laurel School's Center for Research on Girls. Most of the other named schools have a "Director of Teaching and Learning" title (Lawrenceville, Lovett at division level) rather than a center, or I could not verify anything.

### Cited Findings
**Verified centers or institutes**
- **The Westminster Schools (Atlanta) – Center for Teaching.** Danette Morton is Executive Director. The center supports faculty collaboration, cohorts, mentorships, and on- and off-campus PD — [Westminster Center for Teaching page](https://www.westminster.net/catalysts/center-for-teaching); [SAIS speaker bio](https://sais.org/24csspeakers/). An older profile names Robert Ryshke as a former executive director, which is out of date — [Institute of Coaching](https://instituteofcoaching.org/users/robert-ryshke).
- **Phillips Academy Andover – Tang Institute.** The team page lists Eric Roland as Precourt Director of Partnerships and Interim Director. A letter he signed as "Interim Currie Family Director" refers to Andy Housiaux's six years as director (Housiaux was appointed in 2018), which suggests Housiaux left around 2024. I found no 2026 announcement of a permanent director — [Tang team page](https://tanginstitute.andover.edu/about/our-team); [Tang letter](https://t.e2ma.net/message/p0ni47/xyjw9wn); [Andover 2018 announcement](https://andover.edu/news/2018/housiaux-named-next-leader-of-the-tang-institute). **This is a leadership transition; confirm before outreach.**
- **Sidwell Friends (DC) – SFS Center for Teaching and Learning.** It exists and came out of the strategic plan ("Create a center for teaching and learning…") — [Sidwell CTL page](https://www.sidwell.edu/about/working-at-sfs/professional-development); [Sidwell strategic plan](https://www.sidwell.edu/about/strategic-plan/imagine-the-future-of-learning). Eve Eaton is listed as one of the CTL's Coordinators and spoke at WISIT 2026. No director name was found — [WISIT 2026 sched](https://wisit2026.sched.com/speaker/eve_eaton.29mx9ien).
- **Mount Vernon School (Atlanta) – MVIFI, now Mount Vernon Ventures.** A school blog post says Mount Vernon Ventures, "previously recognized as" MVIFI, launched and is "Led by Executive Director Alex Blumencranz." The post is from about 2024 and I could not confirm the role in 2026 — [Mount Vernon blog](https://mountvernonschool.org/blog/category/mv-ventures). The leadership page lists Jared Colley as Chief Innovation Officer and Nicole Martin as Chief Learning Officer (summary of [leadership page](https://mountvernonschool.org/leadership/)). Bo Adams appears only in an older Ashoka profile as Chief Learning and Innovation Officer, which is out of date — [Ashoka](https://develop.ashoka.org/en/network-member/changemaker-school/mount-vernon-presbyterian-school).
- **Laurel School (Shaker Heights, OH) – Center for Research on Girls (LCRG).** It uses a co-director model: Tori Cordiano (Director of Research), Grace Willer and Rachel Herlein. Lisa Damour was the earlier executive director — [LCRG Cordiano](https://lcrg.laurelschool.org/list-detail?pk=210662); [LCRG Herlein](https://lcrg.laurelschool.org/list-detail?pk=210668); [Ideastream 2020](https://www.ideastream.org/news/health-science/2020-03-02/exploradio-how-research-is-helping-stressed-out-girls-build-resilience). It is research on girls' wellbeing, not writing or AI, so it is a weak fit.
- **Beaver Country Day (MA) – Research + Design Center.** Its director reports to the Director of Teaching, Learning, and Innovation (Kader Adjout) — [Beaver job posting via EdTech Recruiting](https://www.edtechrecruiting.com/node/19987); [Beaver directory](https://bcdschool.org/contact/kader-adjout/).
- **The Nueva School (CA) – Innovative Learning Conference (ILC).** This is a conference, not a center. The 2026 ILC, "EnlightenEd: Shifting Paradigms for Living and Learning," ran March 11–13, 2026. No named director; the public contact is the ILC page — [Nueva ILC page](https://www.nuevaschool.org/about/conferences-and-institutes/innovative-learning-conference); [ILC 2026 sched](https://nuevailc2026.sched.com/list/descriptions).
- **Ransom Everglades (FL) – Summer Institute for Teachers.** An externally facing institute "welcoming faculty, administrators and private tutors" for two days of seminars taught by RE faculty, now past its second annual run. The 2025 contact was Matthew Helmers — [RE Summer Institute page](https://www.ransomeverglades.org/summer-institute-for-teachers); [RE news](https://ransomeverglades.org/news-detail?pk=1342594).

**Title-only (no named center found)**
- **Lawrenceville:** Craig Cetrulo is listed as Director of Teaching and Learning in the Office of the Dean of Academics. The source is the directory plus a 2022 job posting, so the listing may be stale — [Lawrenceville directory](https://www.lawrenceville.org/directory); [LinkedIn job post](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/director-of-teaching-and-learning-at-the-lawrenceville-school-3002393431).
- **Lovett School:** It has division-level directors of teaching and learning (Amy Darsey, K–2; Jen Tatasciore, 3–5) in an undated article — [Know Atlanta](https://www.knowatlanta.com/education/through-our-teachers-eyes).
- **Hawken:** The only related unit found is the Sally & Bob Gries Center for Experiential and Service Learning, which is not a T&L center — [Wikipedia: Hawken School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawken_School).
- **Harpeth Hall:** I found the Bullard Bright IDEA Lab (a space) but no T&L director — [ISNA member page](http://www.nashvilleindependentschools.org/new-page-65).

### Inferences
- Two different structures exist. A "named center with an executive director" (Westminster, the Tang Institute, and the CTTL/EA CTL models) usually carries an outward-facing mandate. A "Director of T&L" title (Lawrenceville, Ransom Everglades, Holland Hall, Beaver) is mostly inward-facing unless the role description says otherwise. Beaver's 2021 role announcement explicitly includes external partnerships and representing the school "in wider education conversations," which makes Adjout the clearest outward-facing case in the title-only group — [Beaver announcement](https://bcdschool.org/2021/07/beaver-creates-new-role-focused-on-teaching-learning-and-innovation/).
- Westminster's Center for Teaching is the closest analogue to the EA CTL found in the Southeast. Westminster hosted SAIS's "LEAD AI" conference on Nov 9, 2026 (see Q3), so it is an AI convening venue as well.

### Gaps
- I could not verify a named center or a named director at: Lakeside, Greenhill, Phillips Exeter, Hotchkiss, Holton-Arms, Pace Academy, Blake, Head-Royce, Crystal Springs Uplands, Charles Wright, Germantown Academy, or Haverford. Combined searches returned nothing relevant, and individual school sites could not be fetched. **Do not assume these exist.**
- Sidwell CTL director: not found (only a coordinator).
- Tang Institute permanent director for 2026: not found.
- Mount Vernon Ventures leadership in 2026: not confirmed.

## Q2. Which of these leaders write newsletters, blogs or Substacks, host podcasts, or convene peers?

### Takeaway
The independent-school leaders with the most measurable reach are newsletter writers who are not center directors: Eric Hudson (Learning on Purpose, 4,000+ subscribers), Stephen Fitzpatrick (Teaching in the Age of AI, about 6,000–9,300, figures conflict), and Peter Nilsson (The Educator's Notebook, size unverified). The main peer-convening bodies for academic leaders are the Association for Academic Leaders (run by One Schoolhouse), ATLIS (podcast), regional associations, and Toddle, an edtech vendor that hosts "ALF" academic-leader meetups.

## Q3. Which are known for work on AI in schools or on writing instruction?

### Takeaway
The most AI-and-writing-relevant voices are Eric Hudson (who published six principles for "AI and the Teaching of Writing"), Nick Potkalitsky (who argues AI exposes problems in post-Common Core writing instruction; note his job change to a public education service center), and Stephen Fitzpatrick. Inside schools, Westminster (Atlanta) is notable: it hosted SAIS's LEAD AI conference with an English teacher who is also its Innovation Coordinator, Stephen Addcox. The national mood includes some skepticism about edtech: the NAIS Thrive 2026 "Educators over EdTech, Pedagogy over Platforms" session.

### Cited Findings
- **Eric Hudson:** wrote "AI and the Teaching of Writing" (April 2025, "Six core principles"), the "Back to School With AI" series (Year Three, July 2025), and "What My AI Use Looks Like Right Now" (Feb 2026, arguing for transparency in AI use). He gave a June 2025 conference session on the same topic, and Albuquerque Academy hosted him for two days with its AI Task Force (Feb 19–20, 2026) — [Learning on Purpose archive](https://erichudson.substack.com/archive); [STLinSTL25 session](https://stlinstl25.sched.com/event/1shTO/ai-and-the-teaching-of-writing); [ISASW repost](https://www.isasw.org/news/what-my-ai-use-looks-like-right-now); [Albuquerque Academy](https://www.aa.edu/2026/02/27/navigating-ai-in-education/). In a podcast he said he left GOA (where he ended as Chief Program Officer) "about two years ago" to consult — [Buzzsprout interview](https://www.buzzsprout.com/220519/episodes/18538585-de-mystifying-ai-with-eric-hudson); [ADVIS speaker page](https://www.advis.org/speaker-gallery-public/eric--hudson).
- **Nick Potkalitsky:** argues that AI "exposes fundamental problems" in how writing has been taught since Common Core and proposes "writing situations." He co-designed an "AI Theory and Composition" class at his school and is co-authoring *AI in Education: A Roadmap to Teacher-Led Transformation* with Mike Kentz. He is an NAIS author and gave a Grammarly session, "Reading AI Differently: Literacy, Infrastructure, and the Future of Writing Instruction." **Job change:** an earlier profile lists him as an English teacher at The Miami Valley School (independent, Dayton); a newer source calls him AI Specialist at the Educational Service Center of Central Ohio — [NAIS author](https://nais.org/authors/nick-potkalitsky); [Grammarly session](https://experiences.grammarly.com/session-2); [ESC Central Ohio blog](https://www.escco.org/our-agency/esc-blog/default-board-post-page-dbpp/~board/esc-blog/post/beyond-algorithms-preparing-students-for-the-human-ai-workplace); [Substack comments](https://nickpotkalitsky.substack.com/p/three-key-takeaways-for-teachers/comments).
- **Stephen Fitzpatrick:** received a school research grant to explore AI's educational uses and writes "less hype and more reality" for educators and school leaders — [Teaching in the Age of AI](https://fitzyhistory.substack.com/); [AI Proof podcast](https://aiproof.transistor.fm/episodes/stephen-fitzpatrick-how-ai-is-transforming-education).
- **Westminster Schools / SAIS "LEAD AI: Timely Challenge Conference":** a one-day AI intensive at Westminster on Nov 9, 2026. Speakers included Stephen Addcox (Upper School English Teacher & Innovation Coordinator, Westminster), Emily Weinstein (Harvard Center for Digital Thriving) and David Cummings (Atlanta Ventures) — [SAIS event](https://sais.org/event/2026-timely-challenge-conference-lead-ai/); [SAIS registration](https://account.sais.org/events/26leadai). Danette Morton has presented on assessment and feedback at SAIS — [SAIS assessment & feedback](https://sais.org/assessment-feedback/).
- **Jared Colley (Mount Vernon):** presented the SAIS session deck "Artificial Intelligence: What's your position, not your policy" (2023) — [SAIS PDF](https://sais.org/app/uploads/2023/06/Artificial-Intelligence_-Whats-your-position-not-your-policy_53.pdf).
- **Douglas Kiang (Menlo School):** Menlo offers a freshman AI literacy and fluency course, and Kiang teaches students how AI detectors work and can be evaded — [Tom's Takes](https://tomdaccordai.substack.com/p/douglas-kiang-is-teaching-for-the).
- **Hathaway Brown (OH):** the Toddle 100 list includes a "Director of AI in Teaching and Learning" there. I could not find the name — [Toddle Meet The 100 (via search)](https://www.toddleapp.com/the-100/meet-the-100/).
- **NAIS signals:** "NAIS Research: 2026 AI in Independent Schools Report" (member-gated, Oct 2026) — [NAIS](https://www.nais.org/resource-center/resources/research/nais-research-2026-ai-in-independent-schools-report). A May 2026 Independent Ideas piece describes academic leaders reading John Warner's *More than Words* during a 9th-grade English review — [NAIS](https://www.nais.org/resource-center/independent-ideas/may-2026/change-with-intention-building-a-culture-of-adaptability). A July 2026 piece promotes process-over-product writing (reflection, multi-draft revision) — [NAIS](https://www.nais.org/resource-center/independent-ideas/july-2026/when-ai-holds-up-the-mirror). Thrive 2026 included a Signature Experience titled "Educators over EdTech, Pedagogy over Platforms, Teacher Agency" — [NAIS Thrive 2026](https://www.nais.org/events/annual-conference/program/signature-experiences/thrive-2026-signature-experience-educators-over-edtech,-pedagogy-over-platforms-teacher-agency).
- **Ransom Everglades:** five RE English teachers presented at national conferences, including NCTE 2025, alongside Assistant Director of T&L Matthew Helmers — [RE news](https://www.ransomeverglades.org/news-detail?pk=1403760&nc=16568&fromId=278674).

### Inferences
- The opinion leaders favor process-based writing, teacher agency, AI transparency and design over detection (Hudson, Potkalitsky, NAIS pieces, the Thrive session). YAWP!'s scaffolded prewriting, thesis and CER builders fit that framing better than "AI writes or grades for you." Lead with teacher-in-the-loop design and process visibility. Treat the Grading Assistant carefully with this audience.
- Westminster (Morton plus Addcox) is the strongest school-based ELA-plus-AI pairing found. Addcox, an English teacher and innovation coordinator, is a natural pilot champion.

- I could not identify the Hathaway Brown AI director.
- I could not read Hudson's six principles in full (page fetch blocked).
