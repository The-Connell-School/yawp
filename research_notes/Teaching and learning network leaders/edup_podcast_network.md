# EdUp podcast network: guest-fit scan for Brian (YAWP!)

Researched 2026-11-27. Purpose: pick EdUp shows and hosts that Kevin can introduce Brian to (warm intros, not cold pitches).

**How this was researched:** web search results only. Direct page fetches of edupexperience.com and podcasts.apple.com were blocked in this session, so episode dates and counts come from search snippets and aggregator listings (Spotify, iHeart, Podimo, Podscan, Podcast Addict). Anything marked **(unverified)** needs a check on the live show page before Kevin reaches out.

## Network basics (verified)

- **The EdUp Experience** was co-founded by **Elvin Freytes** (co-founder and co-producer, frequent host) and **Dr. Joe Sallustio** (co-founder and host). Both were still hosting in 2026, including at InsightsEDU in February and ASU+GSV in April.
- The network calls itself a **"Higher Education Media Company."** Its shows page lists **31 shows**. Some are EdUp-branded, and others are affiliates that their own hosts own and run. K-12 is a small part of the network.
- Other hosts named on the flagship at various points: Elizabeth Leiba and Dr. Jodi Blinco (current roles unverified).
- In 2026 the network added "EdUp Leadership," described as an archive or speaker resource built from its conversations with college presidents. It also sponsors HigherEd PodCon (details unverified).

## Ranked list (best fit first)

### (Excluded) EdUp K-12
- This is Kevin's own show (Kevin is on the YAWP! team), so it is not an outreach target. Kevin's EdUp connection is how Brian gets introduced to the shows below.

### 2. The EdUp Experience (flagship), especially its Academic Integrity Series
- **Hosts:** Elvin Freytes (host of the series episodes) with co-host **Thomas Fetsch**, CEO of Integrity4Education, the series sponsor. Joe Sallustio hosts many other flagship episodes.
- **Focus and audience:** higher-ed leaders and edtech. The series covers AI and academic integrity.
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** Episodes ran all year, the latest seen dated about Nov 23, 2026. The series ran from at least Nov 2025 to Jan 2026, and whether it's still running is unverified.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** **yes, often.** Examples: France Hoang (BoodleBox), Rusty Cowher (Scholarly), Samyr Qureshi (Knack), Gavin Cooney (Learnosity), and Katie Boody Adorno (ep. 497, "How can edtech help teachers").
- **AI or writing episodes:**
  - Jenny Maxwell, Head of Education at Superhuman (formerly Grammarly), on what the rebrand means for students and faculty (Jan 11, 2026).
  - **Casey Cuny**, 2024 California Teacher of the Year, who teaches 10th-grade honors English and also teaches at National University, on teaching in the age of AI (Dec 22, 2025).
  - Shouvik Paul (Copyleaks).
  - Episodes on treating "AI as a design issue, not a policing problem."
- **Episode angle:** "Integrity by design: building an AI writing tool that won't write the essay, and gives teachers the final say on feedback." It could be pitched as a K-12 view on the college writing pipeline.
- **Caveat:** the audience is higher ed. Pitch it as "what's coming to your campus from high school English classrooms."

### 3. The EdUp Learning & Development Podcast (formerly EdUp EdTech)
- **Host:** Holly Owens, a former high school teacher who also teaches at Touro's Graduate School of Technology and works at Yellowdig. Nadia Johnson co-hosted the EdUp EdTech era; whether she still does is unverified.
- **Focus and audience:** edtech, instructional design and L&D. Mostly adult learning and higher ed, but the edtech community listens.
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** Ep. 194, with Panos Siozos (LearnWorlds CEO) on AI for instructional designers, is dated about Sep 28, 2026.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** **yes, its core format.** Examples: Shaunak Roy (Yellowdig CEO), Shivali Goyal (Trupeer.ai founder), Stuart Grey (Student Voice founder), Regnault Sanders (Schoolconomy).
- **AI or writing episodes:** several on AI, including ep. 123 "Voices Unveiled: AI and Education" and the Trupeer and LearnWorlds episodes. No writing-specific episode was found.
- **Episode angle:** "Building with teachers: product decisions behind a free AI writing platform that keeps the teacher in the loop."

### 4. Disrupt Education Podcast (affiliate)
- **Hosts:** Peter Hostrawser and Alli Dahl.
- **Focus and audience:** K-12 leaders and educators, CTE, career readiness and future-ready schools.
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** The show is in its 10th year, and ep. 434, "Education Can't Keep Up With AI… Now What?", is dated about May 13, 2026.
- **EdUp link:** an older episode description calls it "a proud member of The EdUp Experience Podcast Network," and a network directory lists "EdUp Disrupt Education Podcast." **Whether it's still affiliated is unverified.**
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** **yes.** Examples: Edson Barton (YouScience CEO and founder), Dan Gonzalez (District C), Rachael Mann (EdFuture).
- **AI or writing episodes:** ep. 434 above and ep. 387, "AI Won't Save Education, But Student Voice Might."
- **Episode angle:** "Writing is thinking: what AI should and shouldn't do in the writing classroom."

### 5. EdUp AI
- **Host:** Jason Gulya, an English professor who chairs the AI Council at Berkeley College and writes "The AI Edventure" Substack.
- **Focus and audience:** AI and higher ed, from the point of view of faculty and writing instructors.
- **Active in 2026:** **not verified.** The latest episode seen is dated about Feb 27, 2025 (with Melanie Dusseau, on AI resistance). The show may be paused.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** **yes.** Examples: Kate Udalova (7Taps founder), Nicolas Gertler (Mylon Education), Manjinder Kainth (AI-powered assessment).
- **AI or writing episodes:** all episodes are about AI. The host's background in writing pedagogy makes this the strongest topic match.
- **Episode angle:** "Can an AI writing tool protect productive struggle? A builder and a writing professor compare notes."
- **Note:** even if the show is paused, Gulya is a high-value contact for Brian.

### 6. EDUP Xcelerated Excellence (affiliate)
- **Host:** Dr. Jacob Easley II. The show is produced by Xcelerated Excellence Consulting.
- **Focus and audience:** educational excellence and equity across P-12, higher ed and community learning. Its stated audience includes "entrepreneurs."
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** About 96 episodes, the latest seen dated Jun 27, 2026 (per Podscan). That episode, with Dr. Andy Jacks, is part of a series recorded at the NAESP National School Leaders Conference.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** sometimes (Equate Education). The show leans toward school leaders and Teacher of the Year guests, such as Tiffani Jaseph (2026 Arizona Teacher of the Year).
- **AI or writing episodes:** none found.
- **Episode angle:** "Equity and AI feedback: a free teacher tier, and timely writing feedback for under-resourced classrooms."

### 7. EdUp Innovation
- **Host:** Dr. Brad Fuster, Provost of San Francisco Bay University (per a 2025 listing).
- **Focus and audience:** higher-ed innovation, AI and digital transformation.
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** Ep. 32, with Pam Eddinger, is dated about Sep 7, 2026.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** **yes.** Examples: Tracy Clark (Building Thinkers CEO), Alex Cortez (Bellwether).
- **AI or writing episodes:** ep. 25 interviewed "Alma," a Socratic AI tutor.
- **Episode angle:** "Socratic AI versus answer machines: what K-12 writing classrooms are learning."
- **Caveat:** the audience is mostly higher ed, so this is a lower fit.

### 8. EdUp Curriculum
- **Host:** Nicole Poff.
- **Focus and audience:** curriculum and instructional design. Recent guests are mostly from higher ed (UMass Amherst, NAU, institutional effectiveness).
- **Active in 2026:** **yes.** 42 episodes, the latest seen dated about May 12, 2026.
- **Interviews founders/edtech:** rarely, as far as the results show.
- **AI or writing episodes:** none found.
- **Episode angle:** "Backward design for AI-era writing assignments: thesis, CER and exit tickets."
- **Caveat:** a lower fit than the shows above.
