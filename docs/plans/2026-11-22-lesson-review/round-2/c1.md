# c1: The Crucible, Day 1 (Salem 1692 and Miller 1953)

The final tie back to the opening question is only one teacher sentence in Closing. Students never set what they wrote in the starter beside Salem or Miller.
- **B. - **C. ## 3. Strengths

1. The opening prompts are well chosen for this play. "Have you ever... stayed quiet?" and "The cost of speaking up" go straight at the moral problem Proctor faces, and the Closing explicitly carries that question into the reading.
2. The pacing of the direct teaching is good: one idea at a time (where/when, what happened, numbers, why it stopped, key term). The undelivered deck JSON uses two real timelines, not date lists (R3).
3. The writing is strong in amount and alignment (38%). The three exit-ticket options (explain a concept, understand the conditions, ask a question) each have teacher-facing "main points / must mention / watch for" notes that are genuinely useful.

## 4. Problems

1. **The deck was requested and not delivered.** The reply says "This deck didn't build", the stack says "No deck yet", and slides.pdf returned 404. The plan still sends the teacher to "slides 3–8" and "slides 9–13". On a context day the visuals (map, timeline, faces) are the lesson; without them students get 18 minutes of oral lecture. *Breaks R7, R13, R2–R4, and Brian's "materials ready to use".*
2. **Known errors were left in and the teacher was told to fix them.** The "Check before class" list correctly notes that HUAC was a House committee and not McCarthy's, that the trials continued into 1693, and that the 19 hangings were spread from June to September. The plan and deck text were not corrected: step 4 still calls HUAC McCarthy's hearings, and the deck timeline still puts "19 hanged" under "Sep 1692" and says "The trials end" in October. The deck's 1954 entry, "The hearings end", contradicts its own 1956 Miller entry and was not flagged at all. Students will copy these into notes. *Breaks R11 and Brian's "names and quotations exactly right".*
3. **Nothing equips students to read this particular play (R1, R4, R8).** No character from The Crucible appears except, in passing, Abigail and Betty as historical people. Proctor, Elizabeth, Parris, Hale, Danforth, Tituba and Giles Corey are never set beside the history. The key teaching point for this play is missing: Miller changed facts (Abigail was about 11 or 12, not 17; the affair with Proctor is invented). So is the term "allegory", which is the whole answer to "why Miller wrote about it". *Breaks R1, R4, R8, R5.*
4. **No primary source or text at all.** Miller's own words (the overture's description of Salem, "A Note on the Historical Accuracy of This Play", or the 1996 New Yorker essay) or a short examination transcript would let students do close reading on day 1. Instead every claim comes from the teacher's mouth. *Breaks "close reading of short passages", R9, R12(4).*
5. **Print materials are not ready.** The plan PDF truncates the direct-teaching steps mid-sentence (page 1, step 4; page 2, step 5), so the "sub-ready lesson plan" omits the content. The handouts put one prompt on each of three pages with 4 lines each, too little room for a 10-minute response and wasteful to print. There are no scaffolds for English learners. *Breaks Yawp! "ready to print and use" and "Scaffolding and access".*

## 5. Factual or quotation errors

- Plan step 4: "Senator Joseph McCarthy... Congressional hearings — the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC)". HUAC was a House committee unconnected to McCarthy, who chaired the Senate Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations. The tool flagged this but did not fix it. (High confidence.)
- Deck timeline: "1954 McCarthy censured... The hearings end." HUAC hearings continued for years (Miller testified in June 1956; HUAC lasted until 1975). This contradicts the deck's own person slide. (High confidence.)
- Deck timeline: "Oct 1692 Court dissolved... The trials end." The Superior Court of Judicature tried the remaining cases into May 1693. Flagged, not fixed. (High confidence.)
- Deck timeline: "Sep 1692 — 19 hanged". The executions ran from 10 June (Bridget Bishop) to 22 September. Flagged, not fixed. (High confidence.)
- "When the accusers named the Governor's wife, the Governor shut the court down." The accusation of Lady Phips is traditional and poorly documented, and growing doubts about spectral evidence (Increase Mather's "Cases of Conscience") were at least as decisive. This is overstated as sole cause. (Medium confidence.)
- Deck: "Refusing to answer meant contempt of Congress." Witnesses who took the Fifth were blacklisted but generally not cited for contempt. Miller's contempt citation came from refusing to name others, which the plan states correctly elsewhere. Simplified; minor. (Medium confidence.)
- The rest checks out: Betty Parris and her cousin Abigail Williams; Jan 1692; Tituba, Sarah Good and Sarah Osborne first accused; Giles Corey pressed; more than 200 accused; McCarthy's 205 claim in 1950; the play opened in 1953; Miller's 1956 HUAC appearance, his 1957 conviction and its 1958 reversal; and the definition of a crucible.

## 6. Fixes to the planner

1. **Treat a failed deck build as a failure, not a footnote.** Retry automatically before replying. If it still fails, remove slide-number references from the plan, and don't print "Here is the deck to project" above a missing deck. (Brian: materials ready; R7)
2. **When the fact checker finds an error, correct the text in the plan and deck instead of appending a "Check before class" list.** Never surface raw JSON. Run the check on every timeline entry, so that internal contradictions such as "hearings end 1954" against "HUAC 1956" are caught. (R11)
3. **On context days for a literary text, require a "the book" bridge slide.** It should set the historical people and places beside the play's characters, with faces (R4, R8). It should also include a "what the author changed" note whenever the work is historical fiction. (R1, R8)
4. **Require at least one short primary or authorial source on context days,** such as an author's note, an essay excerpt or a document, with a close-reading task. Also require an on-screen definition for every domain term used in the direct teaching (allegory, theocracy, spectral evidence, blacklist). (R5, R9, close reading)
5. **Fix the printable outputs.** Never truncate step content in the plan PDF. Size handout writing space to the minutes of the task (a 10-minute response needs a page), group prompts on as few pages as possible, and add a sentence frame to every writing prompt. (Yawp! values; Access)
