**Overall: 3 (usable with edits).** The teaching moves are strong. However, the handout's main text has to be replaced with a real Storr or Jebb text, the deck is mostly words, and the plan runs 55 minutes against a 50-minute period.

**Top change:** Print the real Storr text from the source, or a labeled slot when the text is missing, instead of a Jebb-like reconstruction labeled Storr. Make "0 found" a visible "Check before class" warning.
