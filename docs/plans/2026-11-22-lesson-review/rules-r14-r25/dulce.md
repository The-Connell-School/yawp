**Overall: 3 (usable with edits).** The close reading and the irony mini-lesson are well built. The timeline has a wrong date, the map is wrong, there are no pictures of the war, and the plan and deck run 55 minutes against a 50-minute period.

**Top change:** Show the Western Front: add a world slide with trench and gas-attack photographs, and use a map that pins the Somme and Edinburgh instead of only Ypres. Correct the "Jan 1917 The Somme" event at the same time.
