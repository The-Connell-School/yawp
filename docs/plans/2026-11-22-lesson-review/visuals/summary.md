# Visuals test, Nov 23

Page with the slides, before and after: https://claude.ai/artifact/W1pc1cz4N4PqMb9VU99EYg

## What changed

Six builds from the visuals plan, all live on the preview:

- **The lookup.** The size floor is 320 pixels, so old portraits pass. Each subject gets one picture and no picture appears on two slides. When the lead picture fails, Commons is searched with what the slide says the picture should show, and the checker chooses or refuses. The checker now keeps art of a scene and refuses metaphors and modern stand-ins.
- **Picture kits for 47 works.** Each lists the author and five to eight pictures of the work's world. The picture editor chooses from the kit first.
- **The author on the title slide,** set by code.
- **Diagrams drawn by Yawp!.** The parts of a poem or speech, a story inside a story, and who wants what. They look the same in Present, PowerPoint and PDF.
- **The passage, marked.** Up to four phrases on an excerpt slide are highlighted in the colors of the tasks beside them.
- **The instructions:** the right visual for each job, specific subjects rather than categories, and never a metaphor.

## The results

The same eleven lessons as before, run again and scored by fresh reviewers on the same rubric. Romeo and Juliet failed twice with a server error, so it is not scored.

- **Visuals rose from 1.82 to 2.64.** Eight lessons went up and none went down. *Frankenstein* went from 1 to 3; *Their Eyes Were Watching God* and *A Raisin in the Sun* each went from 1 to 2.
- **What the slides now hold:** 5 diagrams, 14 marked passages, 9 maps or timelines, and the author's face in 9 of 11 lessons. No picture was repeated.
- **The overall score fell from 2.82 to 2.64.** Two lessons dropped a point, *Things Fall Apart* and *Their Eyes Were Watching God*, and both for wrong facts and a missing text, not for their visuals. With one run per lesson and new reviewers, a one-point move in a single lesson is within the noise. The visuals work did not raise the overall score.

## What I got wrong, or have not fixed yet

- **There are fewer photographs than before, 26 slides down to 18.** The visuals score went up because of diagrams, marked passages and authors, not photographs. The stricter checker and the rule of one picture per subject removed wrong pictures but also some right ones, and the lookup still misses most of what it asks for.
- **Why the pictures are missed:**
  - 14 were "not the thing": a general article such as *Igbo people*, *Long Island* or *Harlem Renaissance*.
  - 11 found no article. Most of these were timeline events searched by their phrase, such as "Marches begin" and "King arrested".
  - 6 were "not a picture": places whose lead image is a map, and Shakespeare.
- **The Shakespeare miss** came from my own safety rule. For *Night*, I had refused every file with a copyright claim, and the Chandos portrait carries the museums' standard claim on scans of old paintings. A claim on a scan of a work made before 1900 is now allowed; any other claim is still refused. This is fixed but not yet verified on the preview.
- **I can't tell whether the Commons second try ran.** It filled 1 subject, and the report counted only successes. It now also reports what it tried and what the checker was shown.
- **Diagrams carry the lesson's errors.** The *Hamlet* speech map shows three different sets of line ranges across plan, deck and handout. The *Dulce* diagram has wrong stanza ranges. A diagram makes a wrong structure look authoritative, so it should take its line numbers from the source text, not from the planner.
- **Other gaps:**
  - Two maps leave out the place the lesson is about (Eatonville, Clybourne Park).
  - A picture the planner wanted but could not find shows up only in the speaker notes. The teacher never sees a slot to fill.
  - One credit names the Commons uploader, not the painter (Mary Shelley's portrait).
