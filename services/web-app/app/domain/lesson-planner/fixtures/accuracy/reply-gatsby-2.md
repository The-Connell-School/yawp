## Can We Trust Nick?

### Three signals of an unreliable narrator

As you read, look for these three moves. Mark them in the text and in the passage below.

1. **Self-praise** — the narrator tells us he is trustworthy, fair, or special
2. **Contradiction** — what he *does* or *notices* contradicts what he *claims* about himself
3. **Omission** — something he seems to be saying around, or not saying at all
